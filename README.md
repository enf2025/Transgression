# Transgression
Light survival modifications to slow progression, without being annoying or adding major new systems

The name, and ethos for this pack, come from a combanation of *trans* (latin for across), and *progression*. Hence, this pack is supposed to make many light changes across progression. Sadly, half of them were not as cool as they sounded in my head, so the big change is iron being more expensive.

A quick overview of non-iron changes:
* Diamond tools require amythyst shards instead of sticks
* Tridents and Shields are now repairable in anvils
* Iron tools have double durability (and copper is also slightly buffed)
* Echo shards are craftable and can make exp bottles or, optionally, be used in the enchanter recipe instead of obsidian.
* Rails are now much cheaper and can go nearly 4 times as fast
* Shields are a LOT more expensive

Finally, note that I suggest using the villager rebalance experiment with this, just because the villager rebalance experiment is cool and makes mending harder and less annoying to get. It used to be included here, but because a major change, and so easy to add on your own, I took it out. See the changes it makes [here](minecraft.wiki/w/Villager_Trade_Rebalance)

## Additions:

* Made more tools repairable
    * Tridents are now repairable with prismarine shards & crystals
    * Shields are now repairabe with iron ingots

* Echo shards can now be crafted with amethyst and lapis
* You can craft 3 expirience bottles with a bottle and three echo shards
* Enchanting tables opptionally take echo shards in place of obsidian


## Changes:

### Tool & Equipment changes
 * Diamond tools now require amethyst in place of sticks

* Change durability
	* Copper tools now have 250 durability (+60, 1.3x)
	* Iron tools now have 500 durability (+250, 2x)

* Arrows now craft in batches of eight

### Iron:
* Iron Ingots are about three times rarer
	* Iron ore drops 2-4 raw iron, but
	* Raw iron now smelts into nuggets, and
	* Nerfed iron gollom drops to 1-2 ingots

* To account for this, many QOL recipes and also anvils are cheaper
	*  Stone cutters now take copper ingots in place of iron ingot
	* Compasses use eight nuggets instead of two ingots (-3.1 ingots, 0.22x)
	* Blast furnaces have had the iron and smooth stone switched (-2 ingots, 0.6x)
	* Hoppers now require nuggets instead of ingots in all but one spot (-3.6 ingots, 0.28x)
	* Anvils have one of thier blocks replaced with an ingot (-9 ingots, 0.7x)
	* Iron chains now make 3 chains per craft instead of 1 (0.3x)
	* Cauldrons, Iron bars, and tripwires all require nuggets in any space they previously required ingots
		* Iron bars, though, only craft 8 at a time instead of 16

* Made shields more expensive beacuse they're op (+5 iron, 6x)


### Rail:
* Force adds the minecart improvements expiriment
* Normal rails are now crafted with iron nuggets instead of ingots
* All rail types now make 16 rails per craft
* Max rail speed increased from 8 to 22, the same speed as creative mode sprint-flying. (Max horse speed is 15, or 18 w/ speed 2)
